Your rights and responsibilities

Customer rights

  1. Clear and transparent information about loan terms and costs.
  2. Fair and respectful treatment.
  3. Appropriate privacy and protection of personal information.
  4. A clear complaint and redress process.
  5. Freedom to consider the offer without pressure or coercion.
  6. Protection from borrowing beyond what is reasonably affordable.

Customer responsibilities

  1. Provide complete and accurate information.
  2. Read and understand the Key Facts Statement and final agreement.
  3. Make repayments on the agreed dates.
  4. Report account problems or important changes promptly.
  5. Use the loan responsibly and only for lawful purposes.

Money Tree responsibilities

  1. Disclose the full loan terms, interest, fees and repayment schedule.
  2. Treat customers fairly and protect confidential information.
  3. Maintain appropriate internal controls and complaint procedures.
  4. Operate only within the permissions and legal requirements confirmed by the client’s qualified advisers.
How to protect yourself from fraud

Keep personal data secure

Do not share passwords, one-time codes, identity documents or financial details with unknown people or unofficial channels.

Understand the loan terms

Review the interest, fees, total repayment and payment dates before accepting an offer.

Do not rush decisions

Take time to verify any offer. Money Tree should never pressure you to send money immediately to an unknown number.

Report suspicious offers

Use the client-approved customer-care channel to report messages, calls or payment requests that appear suspicious.
